=======================================================
DESI DRIVE CHALLENGE - GLB CAR MODEL UPLOAD DIRECTORY
=======================================================

HOW TO ADD YOUR DOWNLOADED GLB / GLTF CAR MODEL:

Method 1: Direct File System Upload (Recommended for Local Dev)
---------------------------------------------------------------
1. Place your downloaded 3D car model in this directory:
   /public/models/custom_car.glb

2. Supported formats:
   - Binary glTF (.glb) - Recommended (single file containing all geometry, textures, and materials)
   - Standard glTF (.gltf with embedded base64 textures or companion .bin)

3. Refresh the app. The game will automatically detect and load your model as:
   "Custom Exotic GLB" in the Luxury Showroom carousel!

Method 2: In-App Direct Browser Upload (Instant Preview!)
---------------------------------------------------------
1. Open the game in your browser.
2. In the Luxury Showroom, click the "Import GLB" button (top bar).
3. Select your downloaded .glb file from your computer or phone.
4. Your car loads instantly with automatic normalization, 360° inspection,
   custom paint compatibility, and full playable track driving!

AUTO-OPTIMIZATION FEATURES:
- Automatic scale normalization (fits standard ~4.5m sports car proportions)
- Automatic ground centering (wheels sit flush on the floor/road)
- Automatic forward heading alignment
- PBR material reflections & soft studio contact shadow
- Working headlights, taillights, and underglow ground lighting
- Real-time arcade driving physics, drifting, nitro, and collision integration
